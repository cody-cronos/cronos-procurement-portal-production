# Late actual costs and simple customer Excel

For approved material and direct expense Actions, **Expenses → Record Actual Cost** now works after completion. The Action stays Complete. Each vendor invoice produces a separate cost and audit posting; duplicate invoice detection, cost limits, role permissions and immutable history remain enforced. Cancelled and unapproved Actions still reject cost postings.

The Action Ledger's **Customer Excel** button refreshes saved project data and downloads one Customer Summary worksheet. It shows total customer funding, approved spending, funds remaining and the purchase register together. The register includes Action, Description, PO Number, Status, Approved Amount, Action Date and Posted Date. Dates are numeric Excel values; Posted Date uses the latest commitment or release posting in Eastern time. Missing dates are marked Not recorded. Approved spending links to the register total, and funds remaining equals funding less approved spending. Supplier costs, internal transaction detail and margins are excluded. Financial History retains **Internal ledger Excel** for internal use.

The customer projection validates funding and commitments before exporting. Pending Actions do not reserve funds. Expense costs do not increase customer commitments or reduce remaining funding a second time. Completed direct expenses without POs display Complete in the customer summary. No database migration, reopening or automatic posting of production costs is required.

The release manifest is authoritative for this legacy portal. Updated existing files retain their flat upload mirrors; new report modules and tests are installed by the manifest. Run `node atlas-prepare-release.mjs --check` to verify the installed source matches it.
